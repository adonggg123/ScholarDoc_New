SYSTEM OVERVIEW

Empowering Scholars with Fast, Transparent Verification

ScholarDoc eliminates paper-heavy document submissions and long queueing for Tertiary Education Subsidy (TES) and university scholarship programs. Scholars capture ID cards, provide digital specimen signatures, and monitor disbursement status in real time.

Instant Document Processing

Requirements are processed and validated within minutes rather than weeks.

Data Privacy & Bank-Grade Security

Strictly compliant with Republic Act 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012) with encrypted SA storage.

Unified Multi-Platform Access

Seamlessly available on mobile smartphones, tablets, and university administrative workstations.

POWERFUL CAPABILITIES

Built for Modern Campus Administration

Every feature is precision-engineered to make scholarship billing, document capture, and student verification effortless.

MOBILE & WEB

Instant ID Capture

Photograph front and back of student ID cards and draw digital specimen signatures directly in the browser.

AI OCR ENGINE

AI OCR Extraction

Automatically extract Savings Account (SA) numbers, student names, and ID numbers with 99.4% verified accuracy.

LIVE NOTIFICATIONS

Real-Time Alerts

Receive immediate status updates, verification badges, and correction notices without visiting the office.

BANKING SECURITY

Encrypted SA Tracking

CAMPUS DIRECTORY
