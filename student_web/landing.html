SYSTEM OVERVIEW

Empowering Scholars with Fast, Transparent Verification

ScholarDoc eliminates paper-heavy document submissions and long queueing for Tertiary Education Subsidy (TES) and university scholarship programs. Scholars can capture ID cards, draw digital specimen signatures, and monitor their status in real-time.

Instant Processing

Documents are verified within minutes, not days.

Data Privacy Compliant

Fully compliant with the Data Privacy Act of 2012.

Cross-Platform Access

Access from mobile app, web browser, or admin console.

KEY FEATURES

Built for Modern Education

Every capability designed to make scholarship management fast, accurate, and transparent.

Instant ID Capture

Photograph front and back of student ID cards and specimen signatures directly from web or mobile.

AI OCR Extraction

Automatically extract Savings Account numbers and student data with 99.4% accuracy.

Real-Time Alerts

Get instant push notifications on document verification status and resubmission requests.

Encrypted SA Tracking

HOW IT WORKS

4 Steps to Verification

A streamlined workflow from login to payout tracking.

1

Login & Profile

Access the portal with your Student ID and set up your academic profile.

2

Capture Docs

Upload ID front/back, draw your digital signature, and enter SA details.

3

OCR Review

Automated text extraction verifies data against registered records.

4

Approval & Payout

Receive verification badge and track disbursement logs in real time.
