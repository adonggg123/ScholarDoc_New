SYSTEM OVERVIEW

Empowering Scholars with Transparent, Fast Verification

ScholarDoc eliminates paper-heavy document submissions and long queueing for Tertiary Education Subsidy (TES) and university scholarship programs at USTP Oroquieta City Campus.

Scholars can capture ID cards, draw digital specimen signatures, and monitor their status in real-time, while administrators review side-by-side OCR data and generate PDF audit reports effortlessly.
